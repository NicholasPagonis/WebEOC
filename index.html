      About WebEOC

      WebEOC (by Juvare) is Perth Airport Pty Ltd's (PAPL) incident management system. It gives the Airport Control Centre (ACC) and Emergency Coordination Centre (ECC) a single shared, time-stamped record of every incident that needs a coordinated response, from the first call through to stand-down.

      WebEOC or PASS?

      hubWebEOC, when the incident

      • needs a coordinated response (previously a NAG)
      • needs airport communications or resources mobilised, for example Terminal Duty Manager (TDM) support
      • is a Level 3 incident
      • involves any ECC activation

      descriptionPASS only, when

      • the matter doesn't need a coordinated response
      • it can be handled later through normal reporting

      infoSome incidents will stay in PASS. That's expected and isn't a system fault.

      What doesn't change

      WebEOC changes how incident information is captured and shared. It doesn't change who is responsible for managing incidents. Those responsibilities stay as set out in the Aerodrome Emergency Plan (AEP) and existing procedures.

      Why are we changing?

      For more than 10 years, Notification and Assessment Guides (NAGs) and the Perth Airport Response System (PARS) have been used to pass on and collect information during incidents. In 2023 we surveyed ACC Operators and Airport Operations Managers (AOMs) about the problems they ran into during incidents. Their answers made the case for a digital incident management system, and WebEOC was selected in January 2024.

      Your part: incident review

      Business as usual, in PASS. WebEOC has already created the record and filled in the sequence of events, so you're checking and adding to it rather than starting from scratch.

      1. Check your emailWhen the ACC closes the incident, you'll get an automatic email with the PASS INC number. It goes only to the person selected as the reporter.
      2. Complete your reported-by fieldsWrite the summary and add what you saw on the ground. Then complete Category, CCTV bookmark and Incident type on the Fire and Emergency tab.
      3. Assign the incident managerWhoever you would normally set, for example the Airport Operations Manager (AOM). This part doesn't change.
      4. Submit it for reviewSend it to the relevant person for review. Quote the INC number in any follow-up, because it never changes.
      celebration

      Good news: you no longer need to write a time-stamped sequence of events.

      Your updates in the WebEOC activity log fill it in for you, so your summary can focus on what you saw on the ground.

      During an incident

      • You may receive an SMS or email notification from WebEOC. Please acknowledge it, because the ACC can see who has responded and will phone anyone who hasn't.
      • Pass your observations to the ACC as things happen. If you're the main person on the ground, they'll select you as the reporter, as if you'd submitted it yourself. We're investigating a way for you to add to the record before it closes.

      Incidents that don't go into WebEOC

      If an incident doesn't need a coordinated response, log it in PASS yourself, the same way you do now.

      Access and logging in

      You log in through PAPL single sign-on (SSO), and only after you've completed WebEOC training for your role.

      • Access comes from membership of the WebEOC SSO group, which Emergency Operations requests through a Service Desk ticket.
      • Your position (for example ACC Operator or ECC Emergency Coordinator) controls which boards and functions you can see. Positions follow least privilege.
      • Use only your own account. Don't share logins.
      • Before you enter anything, check the position and incident shown in the top-right corner. Every entry is stamped with both.

      groupWho holds accounts from go-live: the ACC, Airport Operations Managers (AOMs), the ECC and Business Support. Everyone else keeps reporting to the ACC and receiving notifications as they do now.

      headset_micACC

      Airport Control Centre

      The ACC creates every WebEOC incident, keeps it current and closes it, which hands the record over to PASS.

      Daily workflow

      1. Log in as ACC Operator under the Daily Operations incident, open Boards and select Incident Management Log.
      2. Create the incident as soon as you're told about it. You can add detail later.
      3. Saving the incident creates the matching PASS incident and its INC number. The INC number never changes, so quote it in all follow-up.
      4. Work through the Response tab checklist for the incident type.
      5. Add log entries and send notifications.
      6. At Level 3, assign the incident to the Emergency Coordination Centre group. ECC activation itself follows EMG-SOP-003.
      7. Close the incident (see closing and handover).

      OpsAdvice now comes from the Alerts plugin, not the incident Activity Log. An OpsAdvice can be sent by SMS, email, voice call or WebEOC inbox.

      Emergency Coordination Centre

      ECC staff work only in the Level 3 incident escalated by the ACC. You maintain the Incident Overview and Objectives (IOO) board and the Information Decision Task (IDT) Log. You don't create incidents, because the ACC has already done that.

      Getting in

      1. Log in and select your ECC position and the Level 3 incident.
      2. Check the INC number against the one the ACC gave you.
      3. Open Boards and the Major Incidents menu. A red "New Data" tag means a board has changed since you last opened it.

      Information Decision Task Log

      Record the information first, then the decision, then the task, so the reason for every decision is traceable.

      1. infoInformationAnything the ECC is told that may affect the responseExample: a forecast wind change from the Bureau of Meteorology
      2. gavelDecisionA decision made because of that information, including the reason and who was involvedExample: keep Runway 03/21 closed until 17:00
      3. task_altTaskAn action assigned to a person or team, with a due time and statusExample: ARFFS to move a tender to the eastern holding point

      Decisions and tasks raised from an Information record stay linked to it and keep their sequential numbering.

      Incident Overview and Objectives board

      This is the single summary of the incident: what's happening, what the response is trying to achieve and how far it has got. It covers the overview, objectives, priorities, issues, key media messages, history and export.

      Recording, handover and outages

      Recording standards

      • Record facts: what was reported, by whom and when.
      • Log entries on behalf of responders and agencies who don't have access.
      • Use Update to add new information. Use Edit only to correct a mistake.
      • Don't delete incidents. Close them so the record is kept.
      • Every entry is time-stamped and kept in the history, which forms the auditable record of the response.

      Closing an incident and handing over to PASS

      1. Once the response has returned to normal operations, the ACC Team Leader closes the incident and completes the required fields.
      2. Set the Reported By user to the person who was on the ground, such as a TDM, Ground Transport Officer (GTO) or Airfield Operations staff member. Don't use the AOM, because they aren't usually on the ground responding. TBC
      3. Closing sends the record to PASS, completes the shift log and emails the INC number to the Reported By user only. Other notification recipients aren't notified.

      lockThe WebEOC Content field in PASS is restricted to the Incident Manager, the Investigation Team and nominated users, because it may contain sensitive information.

      Contacts and notifications

      WebEOC sends notifications by SMS and email. Recipients respond, and their responses appear on the Acknowledgements tab. Automatic phone escalation isn't available at go-live.

      Contact groups

      • PARS groups have the prefix PARS and a number, for example PARS-0001. Other groups include the Medical Incident Immediate Response Notification Group, Airfield Operations and Agencies.
      • Group membership is driven by a contact-group tag on each contact. When a contact's tag matches a group, they're added to it automatically.

      Keeping your details current

      • Emergency Operations maintains contacts in the WebEOC Contacts Manager.
      • Every 60 to 90 days you'll receive an email with a form link asking you to confirm or update your details. Emergency Operations reviews each submission before applying it.
      • Your email address is your unique identifier, so you can't change it through the form. If it changes, contact Emergency Operations.
      • If you're a PAPL staff member with a WebEOC account, you're managed separately to avoid SSO issues.

      shield_personPrivacy. Information isn't shared with emergency services automatically. It's shared only when appropriate to the incident and the recipients.

      Frequently asked questions

      WebEOC and PASS

      Which incidents go into WebEOC?expand_more

      Only incidents that need a coordinated response, which previously meant a paper NAG. That includes anything that needs airport communications or resources mobilised, every Level 3 incident and any ECC activation. Everything else is logged in PASS as it is today.

      Why wasn't my incident put into WebEOC?expand_more

      The ACC Team Leader decided it didn't need a coordinated response. That's expected and isn't a system fault. Log it in PASS yourself, the same way you do now.

      Does WebEOC change who manages an incident?expand_more

      No. Responsibilities stay as set out in the AEP and existing procedures. WebEOC only changes how information is captured and shared.

      Handover and review

      What do I need to do after an incident?expand_more

      If you're the Reported By user you'll get an email with the PASS INC number. Review the PASS incident and add your observations, complete the highlighted fields on the Fire and Emergency tab, assign the incident manager and submit it for review. See your part in incident review.

      Do I still write a sequence of events?expand_more

      No. The WebEOC activity log fills in the time-stamped sequence of events for you.

      Can I add to the record before the incident closes?expand_more

      Not directly at go-live. Give your observations to the ACC as things happen and they'll log them. We're investigating a way for responders to add to the record before it closes.

      Numbers and backup

      When is the INC number created?expand_more

      As soon as the ACC saves the incident in WebEOC. It never changes, so quote it in all follow-up. Every WebEOC incident produces a PASS record.

      I didn't get the INC email. Who do I contact?expand_more

      The email goes only to the Reported By user, so other people involved won't receive it. If you were the main person on the ground and nothing arrived, contact the ACC or the WebEOC project mailbox at WebEOCProject@perthairport.com.au.

      What happens if WebEOC is down?expand_more

      The NAGs remain the backup. PARS is being decommissioned, so notifications will be made by manual phone call.

      Access and notifications

      Will I get access to WebEOC?expand_more

      From go-live, accounts are held by the ACC, AOMs, the ECC and Business Support, and each is granted only after training for that role. Other operational staff won't have access. The ACC logs your updates from radio, phone calls and CCTV.

      I received a notification by mistake. What do I do?expand_more

      Let Emergency Operations know through the WebEOC project mailbox. Group membership comes from the contact-group tag on your contact record, so the tag can be corrected.

      How do I update my phone number?expand_more

      Use the form link in the email you get every 60 to 90 days asking you to confirm your details, or contact Emergency Operations. Your email address can't be changed through the form because it's your unique identifier.
